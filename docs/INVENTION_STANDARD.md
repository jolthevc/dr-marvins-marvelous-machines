# Marvelous Machine Invention Standard v1.0

## The proposition

Every post presents an **imaginary but internally coherent object** that attacks a small, familiar annoyance with outrageously disproportionate ingenuity. The product must be interesting before the narrator adds personality.

Two truths must coexist:

- **The premise is mad.** Nobody sensible would commission a scientific breakthrough to rescue a toast crumb.
- **The mechanism is smart.** A viewer can explain, in their own words, how the object is meant to accomplish its tiny mission.

The formula is **ridiculous importance + real ingenuity + instantly legible benefit**.

## Admission gates

Reject or strengthen a candidate if it cannot answer these six questions:

1. **The everyday trigger:** What exact moment makes a normal person exclaim "I hate when that happens"?
2. **The existing compromise:** What do they reluctantly do today, and why is it imperfect?
3. **The improbable intervention:** What physical object or mechanism would change that moment?
4. **The engineering spine:** Identify at least one credible mechanism and three nonredundant design features.
5. **The poster shot:** Can one illustrated object tell the story without a complicated diagram or a human actor?
6. **The private desire:** Even knowing this is absurd, would some people genuinely want it?

Strong answers do not require advanced software or improbable materials. Clever geometry, springs, tracks, seals, magnets, hinges, capillary action, baffles, weighted bases, rollers, modular inserts, pneumatics and user-interface constraints are often better than science fiction.

## Preferred invention strategies

- **Eliminate:** Remove a tedious step entirely.
- **Capture and reclaim:** Stop drops, crumbs, cables, dirt or loose items from escaping.
- **Reconfigure:** Make an ordinary object transform in a surprising way.
- **Refuse the failure:** Use a passive mechanism to prevent the one common thing that goes wrong.
- **One more hand:** Let someone accomplish a two-handed job while carrying something else.
- **Deliberately overengineer:** A simple mechanism with theatrically excessive controls or casing.
- **Combine two products:** Only when interaction is genuinely new and the result is not an existing multi-tool.
- **Reverse the default:** Solve the problem by changing a commonplace assumption about how the object operates.

## Levels of madness

Use a healthy mix across a slate:

- **Practical lunacy (roughly 60%)**: plausible consumer concept with one exaggerated flourish. People might buy this.
- **Magnificent excess (roughly 30%)**: mechanically believable, hilarious in scale, excessive for the benefit.
- **Delightfully impossible spectacle (up to 10%)**: whimsical thought experiment; explicitly marked concept and never falsely described as feasible. Use only when visual and comedic reward is extraordinary.

These targets are guidelines for diversity, not an exact quota. At least most concepts should be plausibly buildable. A complicated digital app is not a "Marvelous Machine" unless the physical object and unique benefit are central.

## Novelty and originality

A quick sanity check must compare the proposed idea to ordinary existing consumer solutions and the saved registry. A thing already sold in every hardware store is not inherently original. A novel mechanism, unusual overengineering, or a comedy-specific reinterpretation can rescue a familiar category, but the honest difference must be explained.

Do not claim an invention is unprecedented, patented, patentable or actually original in a legal sense without qualified review. If web research is unavailable, report novelty as unverified. Distinguish imaginative presentation from commercial differentiation.

## Avoid these traps

- **Generic product with renamed benefits:** a bag with pockets, a cup with a lid, another charging dock.
- **AI adjective swap:** "smart" or "AI-powered" substituted for a physical invention.
- **Convenience that requires more effort:** an elaborate apparatus whose setup exceeds the original annoyance unless the excess is an intentional and entertaining joke.
- **Incoherent pseudoscience:** silent magnets that cancel all sound, zero-energy motors, magical air purification.
- **Broad problem:** "fix commuting" instead of "wet umbrella coats everyone on the train."
- **Safety-critical promises:** brakes, medicines, electrical protection, infant devices, clinical efficacy, etc. If included, frame responsibly and do not imply real testing.
- **Pure novelty toy:** has no recognizable pain point beyond looking weird.
- **Weak visual hook:** an unseen mobile app, service or workflow rather than an object.
- **Unnecessary cruelty:** the funny inconvenience must not require ridiculing a vulnerable population.

## What the internal invention brief must contain

- **Annoyance:** a concrete one-sentence human scene.
- **Failure today:** normal workaround or accepted nuisance.
- **Marvelous intervention:** what it looks like and what it actually does.
- **Physical principle:** one to three sentences of intelligible, cautious mechanism.
- **Three features:** three distinct interventions that a sketch could plausibly illustrate.
- **Delight:** one visual or mechanical flourish that makes it Dr. Marvin, not an anonymous designer.
- **Trade-off:** one candid cost, weight, power, space, complexity or production compromise (internal; optional in public).
- **Existing-product check:** inspected sources if possible, else mark unknown.
- **Visualization:** exactly one object in a hero 3/4 view; optional small inset detail may be included within that same image.

## Example: the Grand Luggage Hushifier

The annoyance: a wheel that chatters across hard airport flooring.

The common compromise: accept the clatter, carry the bag, or buy different luggage whose noise performance may still vary.

The intervention: carry-on case with detachable soft-tread wheel cartridges, floating wheel mounts, and dust-resistant bearings.

The hypothetical mechanism: softer contact material may reduce high-frequency contact noise; a compliant mount may reduce vibration transmitted to the case. Actual acoustic improvement would need testing and depends on surface, load, wheel wear and speed.

The mad flourish: an absurdly ceremonial "HUSH" selector, clearly indicated as conceptual rather than an evidenced functional mode.

The trade-off: softer wheels may wear sooner and isolation may add complexity. No numerical noise-reduction claim.

The humor: humanity's scientific progress has inexplicably failed to civilize its suitcases.

## Scale guidance

Idea slate categories should vary physical setting, materials, operation, annoyance, naming and comedic framing. Resist generating twenty variants of "self-cleaning" or "silent" objects. The best ten have ten different reasons to exist.
