# Voice Examples and Anti-Examples

These are samples for **tone calibration**, not a proposed or approved slate. Never register them as ideas without an explicit ideation request and duplication check.

## 1. Grand Luggage Hushifier

**Hook:** "I refuse to let another airport hear the entire contents of my suitcase."

**A most dreadful problem:** "For years I have endured the dreadful racket of luggage crossing a tiled floor. We can carry a passenger across an ocean in a pressurized aluminum tube, yet the final fifty yards sound like an overturned cutlery drawer. An embarrassment to civilization."

**Mechanics:** Soft, replaceable treads; an elastically isolated wheel assembly; sealed bearings that aim to mitigate rattling from debris. No numerical noise promises.

**Verdict:** "Would you install my Hushifier, or have we all simply agreed to drag our dignity behind us?"

**Why it works:** Familiar aggravation, bombastic moral urgency, coherent design, specific choice.

## 2. Crumb Reclamation Engine

**Hook:** "A single croissant has defeated our greatest dining rooms for long enough."

**Problem:** A polished table is instantly reduced to a gravel pit by breakfast. Dr. Marvin considers this disgrace an architectural problem.

**Mechanics:** A small foldout crumb channel, a concealed manual brush sweep, a removable collector. An extravagant brass indicator might celebrate one recovered flake, but does not substitute for the function.

**Verdict:** "Would you appoint this machine Minister of Breakfast, or is the tablecloth beyond saving?"

## 3. Umbrella De-Dripper Deluxe

**Hook:** "A proper umbrella should not continue raining after the rain has stopped."

**Problem:** The umbrella has loyally protected you, then immediately drenches the vestibule, floor and the next person's shoes.

**Mechanics:** A fitted sleeve, capillary drainage ribs, removable reservoir, deliberate invertible stand. Its physical mechanics must remain consistent across drawing and copy.

**Verdict:** "Would you grant every umbrella its own drainage department?"

## Three bad alternatives

**Generic:** "The revolutionary umbrella 2.0 offers innovative drip management." This says nothing specific and has no persona.

**Noise without invention:** "A magical umbrella warps the weather and humiliates clouds." This has no credible mechanism.

**Overstuffed faux-Victorian:** "Lo, my esteemed compatriots, behold the ineffable and prodigiously wondrous precipitation collection apparatus of truly cosmic significance!" This is verbose, exhausting, hard to scan and mechanically vacuous.

## Why discipline helps madness

Each comic flourish should be carried by concrete nouns and a clear physical scene. The funniest person in the room can still explain what the rollers, hinges, drains and cartridges do. If the joke buries the invention, we have lost the user's reason to swipe.
