# Editorial, Visual and Integrity QA v1.0

## Mission

A disciplined safety net for a wild creative product. The quality bar should be high without burdensome owner sign-offs or theatrical internal bureaucracy.

## Eight pre-package checks

1. **Instant understanding:** An unfamiliar viewer knows the ordinary inconvenience and proposed physical solution quickly.
2. **Authentically Marvin:** The copy could not be transplanted unaltered into a generic consumer-product account. The indignation is disproportionate, literate, lovable and memorable.
3. **Real ingenuity:** At least one distinct, plausible physical mechanism is communicated. Three listed features do different jobs.
4. **Inventive naming:** Grand, specific, and readable, not sterile startup naming or random nonsense.
5. **One-image viability:** A single technical drawing depicts the object unambiguously; no need for humans, animation, or multiple intricate scenes.
6. **Four-slide progression:** Slide 1 hook -> Slide 2 outrageous diagnosis -> Slide 3 satisfying mechanism -> Slide 4 unique verdict. No two slides repeat the same informational job.
7. **Readable brevity:** Respect copy limits and actual template safe zones. No forced tiny typography or empty buzzwords.
8. **Integrity:** The product is clearly conceptual; novelty, efficacy, pricing and commercial availability are not fabricated.

## Failure severity

**BLOCKER:** missing mechanism; indistinguishable copy from generic ad; duplicate problem/mechanism; unsafe certainty; fictional product sold as real; schema invalid; impossible slide text; missing asset despite "illustrated" stage.

**FIX:** overwritten joke, title too long, repetitive feature names, no specific CTA, visual busy, generic caption, insufficient category diversity, clumsy red notes.

**TASTE:** choice among two good names, degree of Victorian flourish, whether problem is more charming or frustrating. The model can make these calls autonomously.

## Internal editorial review

Do not force a 1-to-100 score on every artifact. Before saving, ask privately:

- Would I show this to a friend just because of the object and problem?
- If all Dr. Marvin copy were removed, is the machine still interesting?
- If all features were hidden, does the problem line still make me smile?
- What exactly changes from Slide 1 to Slide 2 to Slide 3?
- Is the closing question about this object, or generic comment bait?
- Does any invented fact look like historical, scientific or commercial evidence?

If any answer is weak, repair the package internally before handing it off.

## Research and factual honesty

This is entertainment and invention speculation, not tested consumer research. A casual web check can find existing analogs and obvious false engineering assumptions. Never claim comprehensive prior-art searches. For technical claims use soft, appropriate qualifiers, e.g. "designed to reduce chatter" instead of "eliminates all noise." Avoid precise efficacy figures absent testing. No fictional patent grants, universities, qualified doctor credentials or commercial availability.

If an invention resembles a commercially available item, record `novelty_note` internally and reframe the twist. User comments expressing demand are not verified sales or proof of a venture-scale opportunity.

Safety-critical items should either be rejected in ideation or worded to avoid misleading reliance. Avoid practical instructions to make dangerous devices.

## Actual rendering checks (future console)

Do not mark `RENDERED` until actual output is inspected for:

- exactly four PNGs, same `1080 x 1350` dimensions;
- identical brand footer and correct page numbers `01 / 04` through `04 / 04`;
- no clipped copy, empty slots, overlapping accents or tiny unreadable headings;
- exactly one actual invention illustration on Slide 1 matching the proposed physical mechanism, with no imagery required on Slides 2 to 4;
- white/cream background seamless and accurate color tokens;
- CTA legible at mobile size;
- no artifact hallucinations of real brand marks, safety certification seals, fake patent numbers;
- copy consistent with approved packet, including punctuation.

If render cannot be inspected, say so; do not mark QA complete.

## Stage and publication integrity

`PROPOSED`: idea exists. `SELECTED`: user selected. `PACKAGED`: full validated text JSON exists. `ILLUSTRATED`: approved image actually exists. `RENDERED`: four real PNGs exist and were inspected. `PUBLISHED`: actual Instagram release URL/date present and sourced.

Separate model-generated judgment from measured performance. Never promise reach, shares, conversion, or advertising economics. Real outcomes belong to future test data, not generated predictions.
